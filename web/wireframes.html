Kourt — interface wireframes

Every screen of the shipped overlay (web/index.html), drawn as static frames with the same demo data the page boots with. The chain carries all the information; the overlay only makes it prettier. Every figure below is producible by a real kourtv2 court — curve prices, conviction caps, bond formulas, and draw splits were audited against the realm source.

Reading these frames: every action button on the real page carries three paths — a gnoweb $help link with arguments pre-filled (the baseline; works from file://), a CLI toggle revealing the equivalent copyable gnokey command, and, in live mode with Adena connected, a ✍ Sign button that signs and broadcasts. Demo mode renders actions inert and makes zero network calls. Screen 2 draws the anatomy once; later frames abbreviate.

What is deliberately not on any screen: live tallies while a vote is sealed; the inflation ceiling; any “backing”, “redeem”, “cash out”, APR, or return-percentage language; a paraphrased claim title (titles render verbatim, HTML-escaped). A verdict always shows its route. Principal is never framed as a wager.

Provenance: drawn 2026-08-17 from web/index.html at loop convergence (41 rounds, 27 critic sweeps — see WEBSITE-ITERATION.md). Demo figures are realm-producible: prices sit on the curve (floor(minted/1e9)), convictions under the hot-rate cap, bonds at 50%·X̄ with disputes at 40%·bond₀×2^rounds, draw splits exact. The wireframe idiom follows gno_grc20vote/wireframes/courts-wireframe.html; screens 3–5 of that file (argument graphs, folders) have no analog here because kourtv2 claims are flat, title-only sentences.
